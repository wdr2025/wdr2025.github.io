Overview

Introduction to Deep Research Agents

  • Deep research agents can independently search, analyze, and combine information from many online sources.
  • They produce detailed, well-cited reports, akin to the work of a skilled research analyst.
  • Growing interest from labs and companies like Meta, OpenAI, Google, and Perplexity.
  • This workshop provides a timely space for sharing research, submitting papers, and evaluating models in a competition track.

Benefits of Deep Research

  • Saves time on complex information tasks, which is useful across various fields such as science, finance, and policy.
  • Represents a major step toward building AI systems capable of extracting deep insights from diverse knowledge sources.
  • Advances in AI capabilities include synthesizing information, evaluating credibility, and producing coherent analyses.

Challenges in the Field

  • Developing systems that can retrieve information and understand context.
  • Identifying contradictions and making reasoned judgments about the quality and relevance of sources.
  • Considerations around citation practices and transparency in methodology.
  • Addressing potential biases in information selection and presentation.

Workshop Details

  • Includes paper submissions, 6+ invited speakers, 4+ oral presentations, a poster session, and a competition track for model evaluation.
  • Welcomes contributions from researchers on all aspects of deep research agents.
  • Topics include information retrieval, knowledge synthesis, reasoning, and evaluation methodologies.

Call for Papers

We invite submissions on all aspects of deep research agents, including but not limited to:

  • Novel architectures for information retrieval and synthesis
  • Methods for evaluating source credibility and managing contradictory information
  • Techniques for generating well-structured, coherent research reports
  • Approaches to citation and attribution in AI-generated content
  • Ethical considerations in automated research
  • Evaluation methodologies for deep research systems
